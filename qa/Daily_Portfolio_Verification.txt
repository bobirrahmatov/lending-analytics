Interpretation and limits
The August 28 comparison in the preview is explicitly illustrative fixture data.
Production compares actual retained published snapshots only. Missing expected weekday
history stays unavailable. No holiday calendar or automated scheduler is assumed.
New-deal period comparisons use the current snapshot universe, as disclosed in the
methodology. Pending periods narrow first-approval cohorts; they are not historical
pending balances. Access VBA and native Confluence require final verification on the
Windows/internal host; this Mac session tested local browser previews and Python/JS.
